## Design intent
The Rafael Villegas personal brand should communicate human depth, strategic clarity, transformation and contemporary authority.

It should never feel like generic motivational coaching, corporate consultancy stock imagery or an AI-tech brand.

## Core idea
**CONCIENCIA · ACCIÓN · RESULTADOS**

## Desired feeling
- human
- thoughtful
- premium and sober
- warm
- transformational
- contemporary
- grounded
- clear

## Visual principles
- generous negative space
- strong typography
- high legibility
- emotional photography with real human presence
- subtle motion
- mobile-first
- clear calls to action
- avoid visual overload
- avoid empty motivational clichés
- avoid futuristic AI imagery as a protagonist

## Content hierarchy
1. Human tension or question
2. Insight / reframing
3. Path or methodology
4. Evidence / experience
5. Clear next action

## Photography
Prefer real conversations, facilitation, workshops, reflection, leadership and human interaction.
Avoid posed corporate handshakes, exaggerated success imagery and generic meditation stock.

## Accessibility
- AA contrast minimum
- 44 × 44 px minimum touch targets
- readable mobile type
- visible focus
- subtitles for video
- reduced-motion support

## Relationship to eXeT and Funnel-IA
Rafael Villegas is a distinct personal brand.
- eXeT: more emotional, transformational and ecosystem-oriented
- Funnel-IA: more technological, editorial and operational
- Rafael Villegas: the human bridge between transformation, strategy and technology

Shared infrastructure is allowed. Shared visual identity is not assumed.
